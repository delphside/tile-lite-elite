# Seats and invitations

## The four ways to claim a seat

A human seat is created with one of four *claims*; an engine seat has none,
and its engine fills it at once:

| Claim | The seat is filled by | Invitation seen in | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `creator` | the player creating the game, at once | nowhere: it needs no invitation | A create-game request may carry at most one. It is refused on a seat added later. |
| `named` | that registered player, when they accept | their `GET /players/{id}/invitations`, and `GET /games` tagged `invited_by_name` | Only that player may accept. The name must exist, so a typo fails the whole request. |
| `open` | any signed-in player, first to accept | every signed-in player's `GET /games`, tagged `invited_open` | A second acceptance is refused. |
| `email` | whoever holds the emailed link and confirms | its link only | A second acceptance is refused. No account need exist yet, as holding the link is the test. |

A seat is *claimed* once a player holds it, and *unclaimed* until then,
whatever claim it was created with.

The claim decides who is invited only when the game is created. Sending or
resending an invitation later, to a seat of any claim, ignores the claim the seat was
created with and takes who is invited from the request:

- a name invites that player;
- an address invites whoever holds the emailed link;
- with neither, the invitation is open.

## Invitations

Each seat number keeps its invitations as rows in `game_invitations`: sending
and resending add rows. While the game is waiting, only an unclaimed human
seat shows a status (`ParticipantDto.invitation_status`), read from the newest
row at its seat number:

| Row status | Means | An unclaimed seat at that number shows |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `pending` | sent, not yet answered | `pending` |
| `rejected` | declined, or accepted and then withdrawn | `rejected` |
| `accepted` | answered by taking the seat | `not_sent` |
| `cancelled` | still pending when its seat was removed | `not_sent` |
| no row | never sent, as for a seat added at an unused number | `not_sent` |

The status is in the state these calls return and broadcast:

- viewing a game (`GET /games/{id}`) and creating one (`POST /games`);
- adding a seat, removing one and withdrawing from one;
- the broadcast after an invitation is sent or rejected.

These carry none:

- any other state, such as a swap's, a chat message's or the one
  `POST /invitations/{id}/accept` returns;
- `GET /games` summaries.

Removing a seat leaves its invitation rows at its number. The seat that next
takes that number, renumbered into it or added later, reads its status from
them until it has a newer row of its own.

**Accepting** binds the player to the seat and replaces the seat's placeholder
name with theirs. Only a `named` invitation can be declined
(`POST /invitations/{id}/reject`); an `open` or `email` one stays `pending`
until someone accepts it or its seat is removed, which cancels it.

A player's invitation list (`GET /players/{id}/invitations`, for their own ID)
holds these, leaving out any whose inviter's account has been deleted:

- every `named` invitation addressed to them, whatever its status;
- every `open` or `email` invitation they have accepted.

## Managing the roster

While a game is waiting to start, its creator may:

- **add a seat**, without sending its invitation, so several can be staged
  before any goes out;
- **send or resend an invitation** to an unclaimed human seat with no pending
  invitation;
- **remove a seat**, claimed or not, cancelling a pending invitation on it:
  - the seats after it renumber to stay contiguous;
  - the creator's own seat cannot be removed;
- **swap two seats' turn order**, once every human seat is claimed;
- **start the game**, once every human seat is claimed and there are at least
  two seats.

Other calls:

| Call | Who | When | Effect |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| withdraw | the player holding a seat, other than the creator's own | while the game is waiting | The seat stays, unclaimed, and its invitation becomes `rejected`, so the creator can resend or remove it. |
| force-resign | the creator, for another player's seat | while the game is running, on any turn | The seat drops out, unranked and unrated ([RATE-3](4.1.0-rules.md#ratings)). The others play on until at most one seat is left. The resignation is recorded as `force_resign`. |
| abort | the creator | while the game is waiting or running | The game ends with no winner and no rating change. |

**A game with no recorded creator** (one saved before games recorded it) is
managed by:

| Call | Some seat claimed | No seat claimed |
| --- | --- | --- |
| start | a holder of a claimed seat | anyone, signed in or not |
| swap, add, remove, send or resend, force-resign, abort | a holder of a claimed seat | anyone signed in |

Every game saved since then keeps its creator: deleting a creator's account is
refused ([DEL-2](4.1.0-rules.md#deleting-an-account)).

## Joining by email

The emailed link opens `/invite?id=<invitation_id>` in the web client:

1. The page reads the invitation's preview, which needs no sign-in: who sent it
   and whether it is still pending.
2. A visitor without a session registers or signs in, with a banner naming the
   inviter and asking the visitor to accept, whatever the invitation's status. A
   visitor already signed in skips this.
3. An accepted, rejected or cancelled invitation shows as unavailable, and
   the page stops there.
4. Otherwise the page asks for confirmation before joining, naming the
   inviter, so a visitor signed in as the wrong player can back out.
   Confirming accepts the invitation.

## Where seats and invitations differ from 4.1.0

The code departs from 4.1.0's rules here:

| 4.1.0 | The code |
| --- | --- |
| [GAME-9](4.1.0-rules.md#games): a declined or withdrawn seat is spent for good and its invitation is never sent again. | The creator may invite anyone to such a seat again. |
| GAME-9: starting the game drops the spent seats. | A start needs every human seat claimed, so a declined seat is re-invited or removed first. |
| GAME-9: the creator may reorder the seats whatever their state. | A swap needs every human seat claimed. |
| [GAME-8](4.1.0-rules.md#games): the creator may withdraw from their own seat as any player can. | The creator's own seat cannot be withdrawn. |
| [INV-1, INV-2](4.1.0-rules.md#invitations-by-address): an invitation depends on whether the address is verified. | Addresses are unverified ([4.2.5](4.2.5-authentication.md#where-authentication-differs-from-410)), so an `email` invitation goes to whoever holds its link. |
| [INV-8](4.1.0-rules.md#invitations-by-address): a link already taken shows who sent it, and opens the invitation for the account that took it. | A signed-in visitor is told only that it is no longer available. One signed out is first asked, by a banner naming the inviter, to sign in and accept it. |
