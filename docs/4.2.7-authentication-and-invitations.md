## The four ways to claim a seat

A human seat carries one of four *claims* from creation; an engine seat has
none, and its engine fills it at once:

| Claim | The seat is filled by | Seen in | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `creator` | the player creating the game, at once | nowhere: it needs no invitation | at most one per request; refused when a seat is added later |
| `named` | that registered player, when they accept | their invitation list, and `GET /games` tagged `invited_by_name` | only that player may accept; the name must exist, so a typo fails the whole request |
| `open` | any signed-in player, first to accept | every signed-in player's `GET /games`, tagged `invited_open` | the first acceptance wins; a second acceptance is refused |
| `email` | whoever holds the emailed link and confirms | its link only | the first acceptance wins; a second acceptance is refused; no account need exist yet, as holding the link is the test |

## Invitations

Each seat keeps its invitations as rows in `game_invitations`: sending and
resending add rows. While the game is waiting, each unclaimed human seat shows
the status of its latest row (`ParticipantDto.invitation_status`):

| Status | Means |
| --- | --- |
| `not_sent` | no live invitation: none sent yet (a seat added after creation), or the latest row is `cancelled` |
| `pending` | sent, not yet answered |
| `rejected` | declined, or accepted and then withdrawn |

A claimed seat, and an engine seat, have no status. An invitation row can
also end:

| Status | Means |
| --- | --- |
| `accepted` | the player took the seat |
| `cancelled` | its seat was removed while it was pending |

Removing a seat leaves its invitation rows at its number, so the seat
renumbered into it inherits them.

**Accepting** binds the player to the seat and replaces the seat's placeholder
name with theirs. Only a `named` invitation can be rejected; for the others,
not accepting is the same thing.

## Managing the roster

While a game is waiting to start, its creator may:

- **add a seat**, without sending its invitation, so several can be staged
  before any goes out;
- **send or resend an invitation** to an unclaimed human seat with no pending
  invitation;
- **remove a seat**, claimed or not, cancelling a pending invitation on it:
  - the seats after it renumber to stay contiguous;
  - the creator's own seat cannot be removed;
- **swap two seats' turn order**, once every human seat is claimed;
- **start the game**, once every human seat is claimed and there are at least
  two seats.

Other calls:

| Call | Who | When | Effect |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| withdraw | the player holding a seat, other than the creator's own | the game is waiting | the seat stays, unclaimed, and its invitation becomes `rejected`, so the creator can resend or remove it |
| force-resign | the creator, for another player's seat | the game is running, on any turn, as with a player's own resignation | for someone who has stopped responding: the seat drops out, the others play on, and the game ends when at most one seat is left playing; recorded as `force_resign`, and the seat is unranked and unrated ([RATE-3](4.1.0-rules.md#ratings)) |
| abort | the creator | the game is waiting or running | the game ends with no winner and no rating change |

**A game with no recorded creator** (one saved before games recorded it;
deleting a creator's account is refused,
[DEL-2](4.1.0-rules.md#deleting-an-account)) is managed by:

- any holder of a claimed seat;
- where no seat is claimed, anyone signed in, and anyone at all for starting
  it or swapping its seats.

## Joining by email

The emailed link opens `/invite?id=<invitation_id>` in the web client:

1. The page reads the invitation's preview, which needs no sign-in: who sent it
   and whether it is still pending.
2. A visitor without a session registers or signs in, with a banner naming the
   inviter. A visitor already signed in skips this.
3. The page asks for confirmation before joining, naming the inviter, so a
   visitor signed in as the wrong player can back out. Confirming accepts the
   invitation.

## Where seats and invitations differ from 4.1.0

The code departs from these rules:

| 4.1.0 | The code |
| --- | --- |
| [GAME-9](4.1.0-rules.md#games): a declined or withdrawn seat is spent for good and its invitation is never sent again | the creator may invite anyone to such a seat again |
| GAME-9: starting the game drops the spent seats | a start needs every human seat claimed, so a declined seat is re-invited or removed first |
| GAME-9: the creator may reorder the seats whatever their state | a swap needs every human seat claimed |
| GAME-8: the creator may withdraw from their own seat as any player can | the creator's own seat cannot be withdrawn |
