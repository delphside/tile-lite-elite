# 5.7 Roles and responsibilities

The roles the programme works through: what each is for, what it decides, what
it is consulted on, what it defers to, and what it reads. The roles are
generic, part of how any programme run this way works. Who holds each one for
this application is the last section, [Who holds each role
here](#who-holds-each-role-here). What a workstream is, and what each owns, is
[5.2](5.2-workstreams.md).

A role is held by a person or by an agent thread. A thread holding a role is
**permanent** (it lives as long as the role is busy), **for a project's life**,
or **on call** (started when work arrives, closed when it ends). Whichever it
is, its state lives in documents and issues, never only in the thread.

## Reading top-down

Every thread rebuilds its context by reading, so what it reads is paid for at
every start and every handover. Reading is top-down, like walking a tree:
`CLAUDE.md` is the root and is always loaded; a role's reading list below is
the next level; a thread descends into any other section only when the work in
hand needs it. A list names sections rather than whole documents wherever a
section will do. Keeping each list short is the librarian's job.

## Who decides

Everything is reported, and everything lands in the repository.

| | decides |
| --- | --- |
| Claude alone | Process Definition, Programme Tooling and Delivery Tooling, and any issue of type `tooling` or `documentation` in any workstream: triage, decide, build, land, close, and delete |
| the owner | as [above](#owner) |

Deleting a check, script, section or rule that is not earning its keep is in
scope, and needs no more permission than adding one.

**The brake**, which either of us can apply in one sentence:

1. **Fewer programme-overhead issues, and less of the owner's time.** Every
   `Type of change` is counted, raised and closed per week, and read in the
   owner's words:

   | `Type of change` | use of Steve's time | should |
   | --- | --- | --- |
   | `functional` | constructive | be where the time goes |
   | `cosmetic` | constructive: counted separately, credited as functional | be where the time goes |
   | `non-functional` | constructive if an enhancement | |
   | `bug` | poor quality overhead | fall; #71's refactor exists to reduce them |
   | `tooling`, `documentation` | programme overhead, what the brake is for | fall |
   | unset | not categorised | not persist |

   Less of his time is read from what the board reports as waiting on him.
   `scripts/programme/board/board-digest.py` reports both weekly, as the
   `weekly-digest` activity ([5.3](5.3-programme-activities.md)). The series
   is comparable from 2026-09-07; before that many issues had no type. The
   tooling share is an observation, not a target, until the board model has
   replaced the tooling it replaces (#383).
2. **A check that only ever finds defects in other checks is deleted, not
   fixed.**
3. **No new check without a named past failure it would have caught**, with its
   issue number.

The reasoning is on #382.

## Handing a role over

A permanent thread's context fills as it works. Each session reports its own
use, tokens used against the maximum, and the demand manager reads it for
every permanent thread at the `board-sweep` activity
([5.3](5.3-programme-activities.md)). At **60%**, a starting figure to be
revised once readings exist, the thread writes its state into its documents
and the issue it serves, and a fresh thread takes over the role from them.
